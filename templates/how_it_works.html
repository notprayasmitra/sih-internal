How it works

RakshaDrishti turns network-flow evidence into a short, explainable forecast of malicious risk and attack stage.

⇩InputCSV · PCAP · library
→
◫Windows10-second flow groups
→
✦Forecastsix-step LSTM
→
✓Risk viewrisk · stage · evidence
⇩01

Ingest

Choose a prepared CIC-IDS2018 or CTU-13 trajectory, or upload CSV/PCAP evidence.

◫02

Prepare

Validate timestamps, map labels, and aggregate flows into 10-second windows.

#03

Build features

Construct a 51-feature network state with availability masks for missing measurements.

↗04

Forecast

Use six history windows to forecast the next six windows autoregressively.

!05

Score risk

Estimate malicious risk, compromise risk, future state, and attack stage.

✓06

Explain

Review flagged windows, peak risk, stage, forecast context, and evidence.

Evaluation approach

The model was evaluated using complete held-out trajectories rather than random flow rows. The final split uses seven trajectories for training and four for validation across DDoS, infiltration, Neris, and Rbot families. Alternative JEPA, Transformer, and TCN architectures were tested on the same split; the LSTM remains deployed as the most consistent performer.
