SYSTEM APPROACH

How it works

RakshaDrishti turns network-flow evidence into a short, explainable forecast of malicious risk and attack stage.

01

Ingest

Choose one of the prepared CIC-IDS2018 or CTU-13 trajectories, or upload a supported CSV/PCAP file.

02

Prepare

Flows are validated, timestamped, mapped to the common label taxonomy, and aggregated into 10-second windows.

03

Build features

Each window becomes a 51-feature network state with availability masks for missing measurements.

04

Forecast

The probabilistic LSTM reads six history windows and forecasts the next six windows autoregressively.

05

Score risk

Separate heads estimate malicious risk, compromise risk, future state, and attack stage.

06

Explain

The dashboard shows flagged windows, peak risk, predicted stage, forecast context, and supporting features.

Evaluation approach

The model was evaluated using complete held-out trajectories rather than random flow rows. The final split uses seven trajectories for training and four for validation across DDoS, infiltration, Neris, and Rbot families. Alternative JEPA, Transformer, and TCN architectures were tested on the same split; the LSTM remains deployed as the most consistent performer.
